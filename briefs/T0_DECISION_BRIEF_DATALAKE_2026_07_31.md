## Decisions requested

### DL-1. Datalake governance: admit GCS as project infrastructure — under a manifest protocol

**Recommendation: APPROVE with the following protocol (Option A).**

1. A machine-generated manifest `data/GCS_DATALAKE_MANIFEST.md` in this repo, produced by a new `scripts/audit_datalake.py`: every object, byte size, GCS-side MD5/CRC32C (transport integrity), and full SHA-256 computed on download for any object that enters analysis. Status vocabulary restricted to {PRESENT, AUDITED, QUARANTINED, ABSENT} — "VERIFIED" is reserved for objects with a full-hash audit trail, like Euclid Q1's certificate.
2. **GCS is a staging/mirror layer only.** `scripts/fetch_data.py` remains the sole entry point into `data/raw/` (CLAUDE.md rule 2, hook-enforced immutability, `data/MANIFEST.md` as the integrity record). The prereg pipeline never reads `gs://` directly.
3. **Exploratory/confirmatory firewall, codified.** The AlphaEvolve/Vertex evolutionary stream (χ² phenotype fitting over w₀/Ωₘ/h₀/PTA-monopole/S₈-gradient) is an *exploratory* channel: all its outputs stay F5b-labeled ("PHENOMENOLOGICAL CONVERGENCE"), FIT-class, and may motivate but never constitute a test. The prereg pipeline (pinned PREDICTION v2, 9-bin Lyα P1D) is the sole *confirmatory* channel. This is the standard preregistration split between exploratory and confirmatory analysis — conflating them invalidates the error statistics of the confirmatory claim (Nosek, Ebersole, DeHaven & Mellor 2018, *The preregistration revolution*, PNAS 115, 2600–2606; Wagenmakers et al. 2012, Perspect. Psychol. Sci. 7, 632–638).

**Rationale.** The lake is already real, already load-bearing (harvest daemon, checkpoints, Euclid audit), and centralizing large public datasets off-repo is correct engineering (GitHub 100 MB limit caused the 2026-07-27 push failure). What it lacks is exactly what `data/MANIFEST.md` gives the repo: a reproducible integrity record owned by the confirmatory side, not by the session that wrote the data.

### DL-2. The three absent datasets: acquire, or strike from the manifest?

**Recommendation: strike the false rows now; acquire selectively, in this priority order.**

1. **Planck 2018 high-ℓ TT/TE/EE — ACQUIRE (P1).** The `plik_lite` foreground-marginalized bandpowers + covariance are compact, public, and the anchor for any multi-probe extension; every late-time amplitude statement (S₈, σ₈) is calibrated against them. Source: Planck Collaboration VI 2020, A&A 641, A6 (cosmological parameters); Planck Collaboration V 2020, A&A 641, A5 (likelihoods). ESA PLA is the authoritative distribution.
2. **DES-Y3 cosmic shear / 3×2pt data vector — ACQUIRE (P2), conditional.** Only worth holding if the S₈ channel is ever promoted to prereg (DL-3); it is the third independent leg (with KiDS-1000 and Euclid) of the S₈ triangle. Sources: Amon et al. 2022, PRD 105, 023514 and Secco et al. 2022, PRD 105, 023515 (shear); Abbott et al. 2022, PRD 105, 023520 (3×2pt).
3. **IPTA DR2 — DEFER (P3).** Superseded in constraining power by NANOGrav 15yr (Agazie et al. 2023, ApJL 951, L8 — GWB evidence; L9 — dataset), and the PTA channel is exploratory-only here. Acquire IPTA DR3 if/when the channel is promoted, not DR2 now. (IPTA DR2: Perera et al. 2019, MNRAS 490, 4666.)

**Caveat for the PTA holding we do have:** the NANOGrav free-spectrum empirical distribution supports posterior-reweighting fits of new-physics spectra (the PTArcade-style workflow, Mitridate et al. 2023, arXiv:2306.16377), which is adequate for the exploratory channel — but any prereg-grade PTA claim would need the released likelihood products proper, an explicit noise-model statement, and its own pinned statistic.
